Parliamentary Rules of Procedure (RoP)

Precedence of points, procedural motions, and voting thresholds during moderated debates.

Standard: Harvard (HMUN)
Motion / Point Precedence Interrupt Speaker? Voting Threshold Purpose
Point of Personal Privilege Highest Yes (Only for audibility) No Vote (Chair ruling) Discomfort or inability to hear speaker
Point of Order High No No Vote (Chair ruling) Correction of procedural violation by delegate or dais
Point of Parliamentary Inquiry High No No Vote (Chair ruling) Clarification of procedure from the Chair
Motion to Suspend Meeting (Unmoderated Caucus) Medium No Simple Majority Informal negotiation & resolution drafting
Motion for a Moderated Caucus Medium No Simple Majority Structured debate with individual speaking time limits
Motion to Introduce Working Paper Low No Simple Majority Present draft resolution formally to the committee
Motion to Close Debate Low No 2/3 Majority Move immediately into voting procedure
